# Provenance and limitations

Published 2026-10-14 from a private personal research workspace. Actual derived research outputs are distinguished from synthetic tests. Historical scripts preserve research lineage; they require the original private cache layout and are not promised to run from this repository alone.

No licensed raw tick/OHLC feed, personal CV, broker account configuration, credentials, vendor SDK, compiled trading binary or trading account history is included. Published ledgers are backtest outputs, not broker statements. Raw data reproduction requires your own legally obtained data.

All quantitative findings are **provisional research** under the validation protocol. Train/validation/test labels describe local experiments; 2026 was inspected repeatedly across the wider research program, so it is not an untouched global holdout. Searches involved many strategies, thresholds and exits; selection bias remains possible. Public tests validate only the explicitly tested functions.

Profit factor = sum positive PnL / absolute sum negative PnL; WR counts net PnL > 0; zero outcomes are not wins. Expectancy is mean net PnL. Closed-trade equity drawdown includes initial zero equity. Costs and assumed fills may differ from real execution. Neither classification accuracy nor historical PF establishes a tradeable or future edge.
